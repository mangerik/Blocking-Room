## Design Context
### Users
Filmmakers planning scene blocking, character movements and camera viewpoints before shooting. Indonesian interface.
### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, creative. A working director's spatial notebook.
### Aesthetic Direction
User-supplied reference: light architectural overview, pastel room zones, geometric actors, live camera inset. Warm paper surfaces, ink text, terracotta selection, restrained controls, generous 3D workspace.
### Design Principles
- Make the spatial scene the main working surface.
- Keep the observer view and actual camera view distinct.
- Make character, set, camera and timing edits immediately visible.
- Use readable labels and keyboard-accessible controls.
